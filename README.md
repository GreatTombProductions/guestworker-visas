# H-2A Guestworker Visa Tracker

Search H-2A temporary agricultural visa applications by employer name or state. See certified wages, worker counts, housing compliance, and job requirements for 13,000+ employers and 254,000+ workers.

**Live at:** https://greattombproductions.github.io/guestworker-visas/

## Data Source

[DOL ETA H-2A Disclosure Data](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/foreign-labor/performance) — The Department of Labor's Employment and Training Administration publishes quarterly disclosure files for H-2A temporary agricultural worker visa applications, including employer details, wage offers, worker counts, and housing compliance status.

## Features

- **Employer Search:** Type an employer name to see their H-2A visa history, certified wages, worker counts, and housing compliance
- **State Browse:** Browse applications by state to see which employers are bringing in agricultural workers and what they're paying
- **Housing Compliance:** See whether employer-provided housing passed local, state, or federal inspection

## What Is H-2A?

The H-2A program allows U.S. employers to bring foreign workers to the U.S. to fill temporary agricultural jobs. Employers must demonstrate that there are not enough U.S. workers who are able, willing, qualified, and available to do the work. They must also show that employing H-2A workers will not adversely affect the wages and working conditions of similarly employed U.S. workers.

## Important Note

This tool presents public government data as-is. A certified H-2A application means the Department of Labor approved the employer's request to hire temporary agricultural workers. The wage shown is the certified offer rate, which must meet or exceed the Adverse Effect Wage Rate (AEWR) for the state. Actual working conditions may differ from what was certified.
